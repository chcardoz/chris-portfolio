A part of the journey of making something is coming up with ideas. And ideas dont stop when you have found the thing that you want to spend the most time on. Instead they increase in number. Chris Rock is known for generating jokes at every chance he can get. Most of these jokes dont make people laugh, from the ones that do, most dont go into his set. Finally the jokes that you hear have been through a lot. Ideas are like that. Most of them suck, but thats fine. You still need to be generating ideas. And to tell you how stupid some of them can be, I thought I would take it upon myself to expose some of my own. Fair warning, some of these are really shit. 19th Century River thames level shit. 

I won't bore you with like every idea possible but will show you some of the themes that keep crossing my mind. This analysis was partly done using my buddy Claudius Maximus. 

The first theme that I keep running into is the lack of patience. I hate waiting for stuff. And you can see this in ideas that I have of a laser powered charging, which I actually like submitted to YC as an applicaiton. I dont like cables to charge with their like different configurations and standards and charging rates. Ugh its so bad. I also dont like how the current financial system is so fragmented. I hate it that I have to pay so many different card companies with so many different accounts and move money between banks with this old ui and broken system. Also paying and managing taxes is whole another beast. I am not against paying taxes but having to go through all of that paperwork is torture. I hope that ai agents will rid us of all the paper and forms that we have to keep filling for doing things in the world. A frictionless society without barriers enabled by technology is the dream that I will fight for. 

Next up is emodied intelligence. I think this is where the world is heading towards. A lot of the world is digital and we can have agent navigatign websites and documetns and codebases. But the bigger part of reality is comically physical. Digging a hole to replace a broken pipe, cleaning the house, makign food, delivering goods, cleaning the streets, making things. Prodcution of goods will finally move to distributted nodes around the world which can fabricate and assemble products on the spot just in time. The ultimate goal would be to produce the good right where the customer is. I believe that 3d printing and embodied intelligence will make this happen very soon. Maybe it would be many different types of robots or perhpas a general purpose humanoid. Robotics with intelligence will create abundance for all.

The next theme is about unification. Right now one gets the feelign that there are too many tools. there is a million different apps for ordreing good, meeting someone, doing work, being prodcutive, meditation. Adn there is friction here too with signign up for every app, with giving you data to each one, with being bombarded with subscriptions or advertisements.
